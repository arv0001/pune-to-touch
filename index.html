The Pune Tactile Map

The board is a raised-line map of key Pune areas. Regions and stations use distinct textures, Braille labels and tactile icons. Bus, metro and auto routes use different line styles so players can identify them by touch.

How to PlayAccessibility

Objective and Setup

Objective: Plan and follow a route on the tactile map to reach a moving target using transport modes, tactile clues and route changes.

1
Place the board.
Put the board on a stable surface.
2
Choose a piece.
Each player chooses a tactile player piece.
3
Draw cards.
Draw a destination card and transport-mode card.
4
Plan and move.
Trace valid routes and identify stations by touch.
5
Adapt.
If a detour appears, find an alternative route.
Example turn

A player receives Koregaon Park as the destination and a bus card. They trace the available raised bus route, identify stations, move their tactile piece and respond to any detour card.

Designed for Accessibility

For blind players

Braille, raised borders, tactile icons and textured zones provide physical information.

For low-vision players

Large text, strong contrast and clear spacing support easier reading.

For screen readers

Semantic headings, landmarks and descriptive labels support assistive technology.

For keyboard users

Navigation and controls can be operated without a mouse.

Real-Life Skills You Practice

Spatial awareness

Decision-making

Adapting to change
